Strength — 3 days a week (Level 1)

Do the mobility warm-up first. Rest 60–90 sec between sets. Level up a move once it's easy for both sets with clean form — not on a fixed date.

Chair squats 8–10 × 2 sets

Setup: stand in front of a sturdy chair, feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out.

Move: push your hips back and bend your knees like sitting down, chest up, back straight. Lightly tap the chair with your glutes, then push through your heels to stand.

Watch for: knees caving inward — keep them tracking over your toes.

Wall push-ups 8–10 × 2 sets

Setup: stand an arm's length from a wall, palms flat at shoulder height and shoulder-width apart, feet stepped back slightly so your body is a straight line head to heels.

Move: bend your elbows to bring your chest toward the wall, then push back to start.

Watch for: elbows flaring straight out to the sides — keep them at roughly 45° from your body.

Glute bridges 10 × 2 sets

Setup: lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat hip-width apart, arms by your sides.

Move: push through your heels to lift your hips toward the ceiling, squeezing your glutes at the top.

Watch for: dropping back down fast — lower with control instead.

Bird-dog 6/side × 2 sets

Setup: hands and knees, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat.

Move: extend one arm forward and the opposite leg back at the same time, hold briefly, then switch sides.

Watch for: hips twisting open — keep them level and square to the floor.

Plank 30–40 sec × 2 sets

Setup: forearms on the ground, elbows under shoulders, legs extended back, balanced on forearms and toes.

Move: hold a straight line from head to heels, squeezing core and glutes.

Watch for: hips sagging down or piking up — that's the most common mistake.

Month 1 progression

Week 1 — Foundation. Just complete the daily quest. Focus on form, not numbers.
Week 2 — Consistency. Hitting the top of a range easily? Add 2 reps or 5–10 seconds.
Week 3 — Progress check. Level up anything you've maxed out for 2 sessions straight.
Week 4 — Build & reassess. Note your new baseline — that's your proof of progress.
