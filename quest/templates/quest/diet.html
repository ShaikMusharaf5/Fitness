Diet

Your tummy responds to overall balance and consistency, not to ab exercises or any single food.

Principles

  • ✓ Protein at every meal
  • ✓ Whole/home-style food most of the time
  • ✓ A gentle deficit, not a crash diet
  • ✓ Water before meals; cut liquid calories first
  • ✓ Don't skip meals to "save" calories

Option A — Plate method (no counting)

Lunch & dinner: ½ plate vegetables/salad, ¼ plate protein (dal, chana, rajma, paneer, curd, tofu, sprouts, eggs, chicken, fish), ¼ plate whole grains (roti, rice, oats), plus a thumb of fat.

Option B — Rough numbers

  • Maintenance ≈ weight in kg × 30–33 (lightly active estimate)
  • Deficit: ~300–500 kcal below that
  • Protein: 1.4–1.6 g per kg bodyweight/day

Sample day

Wake: water · Breakfast: 2–3 eggs or moong dal chilla + fruit · Mid-morning: nuts + fruit · Lunch: 2 rotis/1 cup rice + dal or curry + sabzi + salad · Evening: black coffee/tea + roasted chana · Dinner (lighter, earlier): protein + vegetables + a smaller portion of rice/roti.
